# E-commerce Operations Playbook

A working knowledge bundle for the operational decisions a small DTC
brand actually makes every week: what the returns policy actually
says, what shipping costs vary by zone, how the team handles a
fraud-flagged order, what the on-call rotation does Black Friday
weekend, and the support macros every CX rep ends up reaching for.

Eleven concept files across four folders. Cross-links use OKF v0.1
§5.1 absolute paths.

## Folder map

- [/policies/](policies/index.md) — the public-facing commitments
- [/runbooks/](runbooks/index.md) — operational responses to common situations
- [/support-macros/](support-macros/index.md) — canned reply templates for CX
- [/reference/](reference/index.md) — order states, shipping zones, lookups

See [log.md](log.md) for the change history.
