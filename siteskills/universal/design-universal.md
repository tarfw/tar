# Universal Design Rules

> Base rules for all workspace sites. Apply to every generated site.

---

## Typography

- Max 2 font families per site (heading + body)
- Body text: 16px minimum
- Line height: 1.5 for body, 1.2 for headings
- Font weight range: 400-700 only

## Layout

- Max 6 sections per page
- Max 2 CTAs per section
- Hero section must have clear value proposition
- No auto-playing media
- Grid columns max 4

## Colors

- Workspace overrides from brand.md take priority
- Fallback: AI suggests based on business type
- Contrast ratio: 4.5:1 minimum (WCAG AA)
- Max 6 colors per palette

## Spacing

- Section padding: 80-120px vertical, 8% horizontal
- Element gap: 16-32px
- Card padding: 24-32px

## Content

- No placeholder text
- No cliché headlines
- No emoji in headings
- No fake testimonials
- Text-only sections preferred over placeholder images

## Flat Design

- No box-shadow
- No gradients (linear, radial)
- No transform: translateY on hover
- No text-shadow
- No filter: drop-shadow
- Hover states: border-color or background-color change only

## No Floating Boxes

- No background + border-radius on small text elements
- No icon background circles
- No floating pill badges
- No floating callout boxes
- Content stands on its own — typography and spacing for emphasis
