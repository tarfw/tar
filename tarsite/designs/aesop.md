# Aesop Earth Tones Design System (DESIGN.md)

## Brand Identity
- **Name**: Aesop Botanical Warm Canvas
- **Vibe**: Earth tone warmth, botanical elegance, spacious padding, editorial Playfair serif headers.

## Color Palette
- **Background**: `#F7F3E9` (Warm Linen Paper)
- **Surface**: `#EFEBE0` (Soft Ochre Surface)
- **Text**: `#222222` (Deep Charcoal)
- **Muted**: `#777777` (Muted Warm Gray)
- **Primary**: `#332E2B` (Botanical Deep Espresso)
- **Secondary**: `#8C6D53` (Warm Amber Accent)
- **Border**: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`

## Typography
- **Heading Font**: `'Playfair Display', Georgia, serif`
- **Body Font**: `'Inter', system-ui, sans-serif`
- **Heading Weight**: `600`
- **Body Weight**: `400`
- **Scale**:
  - H1: `3rem` (`48px`), line-height `1.25`
  - H2: `2.1rem` (`33.6px`), line-height `1.3`
  - Body: `1.05rem` (`16.8px`), line-height `1.7`

## Radius & Borders
- **Border Radius Small**: `4px`
- **Border Radius Medium**: `6px`
- **Border Radius Large**: `10px`
- **Border Radius Full**: `9999px`

## Spacing & Layout
- **Container Max Width**: `1240px`
- **Section Padding Vertical**: `96px`
- **Grid Gap**: `36px`
