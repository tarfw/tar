# Lululemon Activewear Design System (DESIGN.md)

## Brand Identity
- **Name**: Lululemon High Contrast Active
- **Vibe**: High-energy, zero-radius square geometry, vibrant crimson accents, bold athletic typography.

## Color Palette
- **Background**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Bright White)
- **Surface**: `#F7F7F7` (Light Neutral Slate)
- **Text**: `#111111` (High Contrast Black)
- **Muted**: `#666666` (Medium Gray)
- **Primary**: `#D31334` (Lululemon Athletic Crimson)
- **Secondary**: `#111111` (Pure Black Accent)
- **Border**: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)`

## Typography
- **Heading Font**: `Inter, system-ui, sans-serif`
- **Body Font**: `Inter, system-ui, sans-serif`
- **Heading Weight**: `800` (Extra Bold)
- **Body Weight**: `400`
- **Scale**:
  - H1: `3.2rem` (`51.2px`), line-height `1.1`, letter-spacing `-0.02em`
  - H2: `2.25rem` (`36px`), line-height `1.2`
  - Body: `1rem` (`16px`), line-height `1.5`

## Radius & Borders
- **Border Radius Small**: `0px`
- **Border Radius Medium**: `0px`
- **Border Radius Large**: `0px`
- **Border Radius Full**: `0px`
