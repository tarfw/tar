# Luxury Black Glassmorphism Design System (DESIGN.md)

## Brand Identity
- **Name**: Luxury Dark Glassmorphism
- **Vibe**: Dark ambient luxury, champagne gold accents, serif editorial headers, glassmorphic card surfaces.

## Color Palette
- **Background**: `#09090B` (Obsidian Night)
- **Surface**: `#18181B` (Dark Slate Glass Surface)
- **Text**: `#FAFAFA` (Pure White Text)
- **Muted**: `#A1A1AA` (Muted Zinc)
- **Primary**: `#D4AF37` (Champagne Metallic Gold)
- **Secondary**: `#3F3F46` (Subtle Zinc Accent)
- **Border**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`

## Typography
- **Heading Font**: `'Playfair Display', Georgia, serif`
- **Body Font**: `Inter, system-ui, sans-serif`
- **Heading Weight**: `700`
- **Body Weight**: `400`
- **Scale**:
  - H1: `3.5rem` (`56px`), line-height `1.15`
  - H2: `2.5rem` (`40px`), line-height `1.2`
  - Body: `1rem` (`16px`), line-height `1.7`

## Radius & Borders
- **Border Radius Small**: `6px`
- **Border Radius Medium**: `12px`
- **Border Radius Large**: `20px`
- **Border Radius Full**: `9999px`

## Spacing & Layout
- **Container Max Width**: `1280px`
- **Section Padding Vertical**: `96px`
- **Grid Gap**: `32px`
