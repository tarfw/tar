# Notion Workspace Design System (DESIGN.md)

## Brand Identity
- **Name**: Notion Warm Paper Canvas
- **Vibe**: Clean, warm, document-first, minimal borders, ultra-readable.

## Color Palette
- **Background**: `#F6F5F4` (Warm Paper)
- **Surface**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
- **Text**: `#000000` (Obsidian Black)
- **Muted**: `#666666` (Slate Muted)
- **Primary**: `#0075DE` (Notion Blue)
- **Secondary**: `#213183` (Deep Indigo)
- **Border**: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`

## Typography
- **Heading Font**: `Inter, system-ui, sans-serif`
- **Body Font**: `Inter, system-ui, sans-serif`
- **Heading Weight**: `600`
- **Body Weight**: `400`
- **Scale**:
  - H1: `2.5rem` (`40px`), line-height `1.2`
  - H2: `1.75rem` (`28px`), line-height `1.3`
  - Body: `1rem` (`16px`), line-height `1.6`

## Radius & Borders
- **Border Radius Small**: `4px`
- **Border Radius Medium**: `8px`
- **Border Radius Large**: `12px`
- **Border Radius Full**: `9999px`

## Spacing & Layout
- **Container Max Width**: `1200px`
- **Section Padding Vertical**: `64px`
- **Grid Gap**: `24px`
